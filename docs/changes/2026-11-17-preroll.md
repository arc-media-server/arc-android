# Pre-roll videos before films

The pre-roll videos chosen in Arc's Settings (Plex's "cinema trailers") now play on the TV before
a film, as they do in the web player (`Player.tsx`; `GET /api/v1/items/{id}/preroll`).

## What changed

- **When:** a film started from the beginning plays its pre-rolls first, then the film. Arc
  decides which clips play (all in order, or one at random) and sends none for episodes, home
  videos, or when none are set.
- **Not played,** as on the web:
  - when resuming part-way;
  - in Watch Together;
  - when the player moves on to the next title (the queue or Up Next).

  Starting a queue with a film from the beginning does play them, once.
- **While a clip plays:**
  - it fills the screen with **Skip** focused at the bottom right, which moves to the next clip
    or the film;
  - Back leaves the player, as everywhere else.
- **Pre-rolls never stand in the way:** a clip that fails moves on to the next one. If the list
  fails, or doesn't arrive within 3 seconds, the film plays without it.

**Code:**
- `ui/player/Preroll.kt`: `PrerollGate` wraps the film's player in `ArcNavHost`. The film's
  player, and its stream, only start once the pre-rolls are done.
- `PlayerDest.preroll` is turned off when playing on.
- `ArcApi.preroll` and the `PrerollClip` model are new.

## Limits

- Not tried on a TV or against a real Arc.
- Pre-rolls play from the file Arc serves (`/api/preroll/{n}`). A format the TV can't decode is
  passed over (logged), since Arc doesn't convert pre-rolls.

## Logging

- **Info:** how many pre-rolls play before a film.
- **Warning:** the list failing or timing out, or a clip failing to play (the player's error code).
- **Debug:** a pre-roll skipped.
- **Never logged:** file names, titles, addresses or tokens.
